# Canadian Change Maker

**Live site: https://musa-ian.github.io/canadian-change-maker/**

Created by **[Ian Musa](https://github.com/Musa-Ian)**.

## Why I made this

I built Canadian Change Maker for immigrants who are new to Canadian money and find it hard to count cash and make change at work. Toonies, loonies, quarters, dimes, nickels, the bill colours, and rounding to the nearest 5¢ can all be confusing when a customer is waiting. This tool shows exactly which bills and coins to hand back, so you can give the right change with confidence.

## What it does

- **Amount mode:** type any amount and see the fewest Canadian bills and coins that make it.
- **Make change mode:** enter the price and what the customer paid, and see the change to give back.
- **Pictures of each bill and coin**, so you can match them to what is in your drawer. Coins are drawn to their real relative sizes.
- **Cash rounding** to the nearest 5¢, the way Canadian cash payments work (pennies are no longer used).
- **Out of a coin or bill?** Tap it and the answer recalculates with what you have left.
- Works on phones, with a large number pad, and remembers your drawer settings on your device.

## Running it

It is a single static page (`index.html`) with no build step. Open it in any browser, or visit the live site above.
