    Bills and coins are simplified drawings, not exact designs. Coin sizes follow the real diameters, from the 18.03 mm dime to the 28 mm toonie. Pennies start marked out because Canada stopped distributing them on February 4, 2013. The rounding switch rounds cash totals to the nearest 5¢. For exact cents, turn rounding off and tap the penny to bring it back. Your drawer and rounding choice are remembered on this device.
